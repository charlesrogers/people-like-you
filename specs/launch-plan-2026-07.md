# PLY Pre-Launch & Launch Roadmap — July→September 2026

**Written 2026-07-15. Companion to `one-year-plan.md` (Q1 gate: 500 members, ≥25 verified dates, ratio ≤1.5:1, north-star <21 days) and `EXECUTION.md`.**

**Hard constraints this plan is built around:**
1. Ad credits become usable **July 22**.
2. Meta requires **prior written authorization** for ANY dating-service ad — including ads driving to a waitlist or landing page for a dating service. Approval SLA: up to 30 days. Running unauthorized dating ads risks account flags that poison the application. ([Meta policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/dating-ads/))
3. Women-first always (one-year-plan §4). Pool today: 16M/6W.
4. No LDS-specific branding in product UX; community targeting lives in channels/creative only.
5. Q1 is free beta — no paywall until Q2, founding members grandfathered at $19/mo.

## 0. The one-paragraph plan

Turn the female-supply constraint into the launch mechanic: **women skip the line** (instant full access), men join a **visible-position waitlist** with referral jumps and a prize ladder whose rewards are all product/status (never cash). Submit the Meta Dating Advertiser Application **today** (7/15) so authorization (~1–4 weeks) lands before or shortly after ads start. Spend the 7/22 credits women-first in Utah County. Nurture the list weekly with community counters ("312 singles in Utah Valley have joined"), and **go live ~Aug 19–20** — inside the 30-day window where waitlist→activation conversion is still ~50%, and timed to the YSA/BYU late-August reshuffle. Launch = admit the first male batch only when the ratio allows, League/Cerca style, and say the density threshold out loud — it becomes the rallying cry, not a secret.

## 1. Why these mechanics (the evidence)

| Mechanic | Precedent | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Visible queue position + "people behind you" + referral jumps | Robinhood | 1M pre-launch list; >50% of signups from referrals ([source](https://waitlister.me/growth-hub/case-studies/robinhood)) |
| Milestone prize ladder, prizes = own product | Harry's | 100K emails in 7 days, **77% from referrals** ([playbook](https://tim.blog/2014/07/21/harrys-prelaunchr-email/)) |
| One earned, single-use "golden ticket" invite per active user | Monzo | **~40% of all 2017 signups**, zero cost ([Blomfield](https://tomblomfield.com/post/691384431502557184/monzo-growth)) |
| Seed the scarce side (women) first via trusted insiders, then show demand side instant value | Tinder / Whitney Wolfe's sorority-first play | 5K→15K users on one campus tour ([source](https://superchargerventures.medium.com/how-tinder-got-its-first-1-000-users-d750a4e04ab2)) |
| Public per-community density threshold before activation | Cerca ("campus goes live at 40 verified students") | 1/3 of Georgetown undergrads; 60K users in 7 months ([source](https://georgetownvoice.com/2025/04/06/georgetown-students-create-cerca-a-new-dating-app-that-swipes-right-on-safety/)) |
| Admission-gated waitlist as liquidity control | The League | 20–30K/city waitlists; only admitted when density supported matching |
| Insider-humor influencer creative | Mutual (Studio C video) | Grassroots → #1 dating app in Utah |
| Speed matters | Waitlist benchmarks | ~50% activate if admitted within 1 month; <20% after 3 months ([source](https://scalemath.com/blog/what-is-a-good-waitlist-conversion-rate/)) |

**Anti-patterns to avoid:** cash/iPad giveaways (attract sweepstakes hunters — junk leads where user quality IS the product); unlimited invites (kills perceived value — Clubhouse's collapse); building the list months before you can admit it (conversion craters); lifetime-free deals at volume (attracts deal-hunters, destroys the $19 founding tier's meaning).

## 3. Waitlist build spec (T16 — needs Charles's go)

**Landing page** (`/` pre-launch mode or `/waitlist`): headline + 3-line pitch (anti-swipe, one real intro a day, nobody asks anyone out — we broker it), gender-forked CTA:
- **Women → straight into existing onboarding.** Copy: *"No line for you. Your face is never in a feed; nobody sees you until we're confident — and you approve him first."*
- **Men → email + zip + birth year → queue position.** Post-signup screen: position, people behind you, unique referral link, prize ladder. (Loss-aversion framing: show both numbers, Robinhood-style.)

**Schema** (migration 018): `waitlist (id, email unique, gender, zipcode, birth_year, referral_code unique, referred_by, position, created_at, admitted_at, converted_user_id)`. Position = insertion order minus referral jumps (each verified referral = jump ~20 spots, computed not stored-race-prone).

**Emails** (extend `src/lib/email.ts`): welcome-with-referral-link (highest-leverage send — ~84% open rate), weekly nurture, "you moved up N spots," admission ("you're in — 48h to complete your profile").

**Instrumentation:** UTM → `waitlist.source`; conversion events to the funnel dashboard; per-source CPL visible before spending a second dollar of real money in Q3.

## 4. Incentives — what we offer (recommendation)

Free beta already means "3 months free" is hollow (everything is free until Q2). Offer **status, access, and locked pricing** instead — the things the case studies show self-select serious users:

| Tier | Reward |
|---|---|
| Join at all (founding cohort, pre-launch only) | **Founding Member**: badge + **$19/mo locked for life when paid starts** (vs $29) + 30-day trial (vs 14). Already-planned grandfathering, now made loud. |
| Each completed invite (invitee finishes onboarding) | **A second introduction every day for a week** (+1 presentation/day × 7 days; further invites extend the week — spec: `specs/onboarding-launch-scope.md` T19). Pre-launch waitlist referrals bank this for launch day. Men additionally jump the admission queue. |
| 5 completed invites | **First date's on us** — PLY covers the tab on your first verified date (~$40 cap). On-brand, self-qualifying, only pays out on the north-star event. |
| Top 10 referrers at launch | Founding Member **free for the first year** (capped at 10 people — scarcity preserved, revenue intact) |

Golden tickets (post-launch): every user active 14 days earns exactly ONE skip-the-line invite. Monzo got 40% of a year's signups from this.

**Never:** cash, gadgets, raffles, unlimited invites, uncapped lifetime-free.

## 5. Meta ads plan (once dating authorization is approved)

**Account structure:** ONE consolidated ad set per audience so it can exit learning (~50 conversions/7 days → at ~$5 CPL that's ~$36/day minimum per ad set; don't fragment). Start manual audiences; move to Advantage+ after ~50+ pixel conversions.

**Campaign 1 — Women, Utah County (80–90% of budget):**
- Geo: Provo/Orem + Utah County radius (geography does the community targeting — religion interest-targeting was removed by Meta in Jan 2024 and is not available).
- Women 21–38, 18+ mandatory for dating ads. No interest stacking — broad within geo/demo, let creative self-select.
- Objective: leads/conversions on the landing page (higher intent than native lead forms: $18 vs $6 CPL but 17% vs 2% downstream conversion in documented tests). Optionally one native lead-form ad set with a qualifying question as a cheap-volume comparison.

**Campaign 2 — Men, small (10–20%):** only enough to keep the male waitlist FOMO real; the queue does the rest.

**Compliance guardrails (creative reviews fail on these):**
- Never assert/imply the viewer's attributes: ~~"Meet other LDS singles like you"~~ → ✔ "A matchmaker for people who date to marry." Describe the product, not the viewer.
- Real people, non-sexualized, no fake "Anna, 2 miles away" profiles, no blurred teaser images, no loneliness-shaming.
- Landing page must match the ad. 18+ targeting always.

**Creative (3–5 concepts, UGC-style, 4:5 / 9:16 vertical, hook in first 2s, kill losers every 3–4 days):**
1. *Anti-swipe*: "I deleted three dating apps. Here's what one real introduction a day looks like." (talking-head, phone-shot)
2. *Women-first mechanic as the hook*: "Your face is never in a feed. You see him first. You decide." (this is both the product truth and the best women-targeted message we have)
3. *Insider humor* (the Mutual/Studio C lesson — culturally fluent, never says the community's name): ward-dating / "everyone in your ward is either engaged or your cousin's roommate" energy
4. *Accountability*: "A dating app that publishes its success numbers. Because we're designed to be deleted — for real."
5. *Founder voice*: build-in-public, why I built this, what happens on day one.

**Benchmarks to judge against:** consumer waitlist CPL $2–10 is the realistic band; dating CPMs ~doubled YoY in 2025, so expect the upper half. Track CPL per creative from day one — this is the CAC dry-run for Q3's <$65 paid gate.

**If authorization is denied:** don't resubmit the same application repeatedly (documented account-risk pattern). Re-apply with sharper anti-hookup positioning; meanwhile credits go to the A2 content angle, and paid discovery shifts to TikTok (dating allowed with restrictions) / Reddit — but organic + referral remains the primary Q1 plan anyway, per the one-year plan. **No agency ad-account rentals, ever** (ToS violation, permanent-ban risk).

## 7. Targets & kill criteria

- **List target by Aug 18:** 1,000 emails (≥55% women). At ~50% fast-admission activation → 500 members = the Q1 gate on launch week, not September 30.
- **Referral share ≥35%** of signups (Harry's hit 77%; Robinhood >50%; below 35% means the ladder isn't landing — fix incentives before buying more traffic).
- **CPL ceiling:** kill any creative >$12 CPL after $75 spent; kill the channel this cycle if blended CPL >$15 (credits are free, but they're rehearsal for Q3's real-money CAC gate).
- **Ratio rule (standing):** if >2:1 at any point, freeze male admission AND male-targeted spend entirely.
- **Launch slips only for:** ratio (not enough women) or product QA blockers — never for "the list could be bigger." Waiting past 30 days costs more in conversion decay than the extra emails are worth.
